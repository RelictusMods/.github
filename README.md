## About RELICTUS

RELICTUS is a community-driven mod preservation initiative dedicated to keeping worthwhile PC mods alive.

Games evolve. Updates break compatibility. Mod authors retire, move on to other projects, or simply decide that they no longer wish to continue maintaining their work.

That does not mean the work has lost its value.

RELICTUS brings together developers, testers, researchers, troubleshooters, and players to help preserve, repair, update, and where necessary resurrect mods that might otherwise be lost to time.

Our objective is not to take ownership of other people's work.

It is to **preserve it**.

---

## Our Principles

### Respect the Creator

The original author remains the creator of their work.

Wherever reasonably possible, RELICTUS will attempt to contact the original author and seek permission before taking over, modifying, or republishing their work.

Original authorship and appropriate credit should always be preserved.

### Preservation, Not Ownership

Updating a mod does not make the original work ours.

Where an original author wishes to remain involved, RELICTUS is happy to work with them. They may retain control of their original distribution pages and publish resulting updates themselves.

### Preserve the History

Modding is part of gaming history.

Source code, documentation, development notes, compatibility information, and previous releases can all have value beyond the current version of a mod.

Where practical and permitted, we aim to preserve that history rather than simply replace it.

### Work Together

RELICTUS is not intended to be a closed development group.

Developers, testers, researchers, troubleshooters, documentation writers, and ordinary players can all contribute.

Sometimes the most important contribution is simply identifying a mod worth saving.

## What We Work On

RELICTUS projects may include mods that are:

- Abandoned
- Retired
- Discontinued
- Unsupported
- No longer maintained
- Broken by game updates
- Dependent on obsolete components
- In need of compatibility updates
- At risk of disappearing entirely

Each project is considered individually.

The fact that a mod is old or hasn't recently been updated does **not** automatically make it available for modification or redistribution. Existing permissions, licences, author instructions, and platform requirements must be respected.

---

## Projects

RELICTUS repositories will contain individual preservation and resurrection projects as they are undertaken.

Where possible, project repositories will document:

- Original author(s)
- Original mod page
- Original release information
- Permissions or licence
- Reason for preservation
- Changes made
- Compatibility information
- Build instructions
- Testing status
- Contributors
- Release information

This helps ensure that the history of the original project remains connected to the work being done to preserve it.

---

## Suggest a Mod

Players are an important part of RELICTUS.

If you know of a PC mod that is broken, unsupported, abandoned, retired, or otherwise no longer maintained, you can suggest it for investigation.

A suggestion does not guarantee that RELICTUS can legally or technically resurrect the mod, but it gives us somewhere to start.

---

## Contributing

RELICTUS welcomes people with different levels of experience.

You do not need to be an experienced programmer to contribute.

We need people interested in:

- Development
- Reverse engineering
- Testing
- Research
- Documentation
- Troubleshooting
- Compatibility testing
- Source-code preservation
- Project organisation

Learning is also encouraged.

Nobody knows everything. Good preservation work comes from research, testing, documentation, and people willing to share what they know.

---

## Attribution

RELICTUS believes strongly in maintaining a clear distinction between **original creation** and **preservation work**.

When we update another creator's work, credit for the original mod remains with its original creator or creators.

Contributors to a RELICTUS project should be credited for the work they actually perform without diminishing or replacing the credit owed to the original author.
